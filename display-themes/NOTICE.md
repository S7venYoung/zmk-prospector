branch codex/kblade-dya-four-themes, based on firmware source ad4ecff.
Existing per-file copyright and SPDX notices are retained.

  https://github.com/carrefinho/prospector-zmk-module
  https://github.com/kbladewht/prospector-zmk-module
- YADS: adapted from S7venYoung/zmk-sofle-dongle-dya, branch 4.1-display-themes.
  https://github.com/S7venYoung/zmk-sofle-dongle-dya/tree/4.1-display-themes
